What the cars measure

The judging engine now separates capability, operations, and economics/deployment. Every metric retains its source type, configuration, date and confidence.

Missing evidence

No unchanged benchmark fairly spans 2021–2026. Operational categories therefore show N/A when a comparable measurement is unavailable. N/A is never treated as zero.

Welcome to Model Grand Prix

An evidence-backed five-year AI model race. Here's how to get started.

1

Explore the Graph

Compare Anthropic, OpenAI, and Moonshot AI across 10 judging categories. Click any point to see evidence.

2

Watch the Race

Click the graph background or press Space to transform into a 3D race broadcast. Each car represents a company's AI progress.

3

Control the Broadcast

Press C to cycle cameras, V for auto-director, 1-4 to switch categories. Use M and N for audio.

4

Research-Backed Stunts

Cars perform visual stunts based on real model capabilities. These are evidence-based, not bonus points. Toggle with T.
